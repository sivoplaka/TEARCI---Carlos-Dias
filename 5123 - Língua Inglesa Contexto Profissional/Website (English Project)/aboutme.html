About Me

My name is Carlos Dias, I’m 28 years old, 
and I’m currently studying Automation, Robotics, and Industrial Control.
I worked at APTIV (formerly Delphi) for about nine years on rotating shifts 
but had to leave due to sleep problems. The following six months were 
dedicated to correcting my sleep cycle and reflecting deeply on what I wanted 
for my future. It was during this period that I decided to enroll in this 
specific course, and I was genuinely excited about the new direction.

After experiencing some depression and self-doubt during those months, 
I finally found a sense of purpose. For the first time in a long while, 
I felt motivated to do something meaningful for myself. Now, every day 
is a challenge, but it’s one I embrace earnestly.

I wake up, go to school, feel excited about what I’m learning, come home, 
study, and go to bed. I’ve come to truly appreciate this routine—it’s 
something I missed and value deeply.
